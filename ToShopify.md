# Features to match Shopify

## High value
- Product reviews & ratings — star ratings, review text, verified buyer badge
- Recently viewed — carousel of last 4-6 books the visitor browsed
- "Customers also bought" — recommendations based on same category/tags
- Wishlist / Save for later — bookmark books without adding to cart
- Stock badges — "Only 3 left", "In stock", "Out of stock"
- Discount codes — apply coupon at checkout for % or fixed off
- Bundle pricing — "Buy 3, get 10% off"

## Medium value
- Product variants — hardcover vs paperback vs ebook on same listing
- Quick view modal — preview book details without leaving the category page
- Sticky add-to-cart bar — appears when you scroll past the buy button
- Email: abandoned cart — remind users who added to cart but didn't buy
- Back in stock alerts — "Notify me" email when a book is restocked

## Lower priority
- Multi-currency — auto-detect visitor country
- Gift cards
- Product comparison — side-by-side specs
- Loyalty points
